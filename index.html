Methodological Rubric

GUIDE AND INSTRUCTIONS FOR EXPERT REVIEWERS

Please review the qualitative orientation and item rating guidelines before beginning.

1

Semi-Structured Interview Script: This instrument is a semi-structured interview script. Questions are designed to trigger broad, deep, and detailed qualitative responses from participants.

2

Target of Evaluation: We are evaluating the quality, clarity, and suitability of the questions to be asked to participants, not testing the participants themselves.

3

Three Methodological Criteria: Evaluate each interview question across: Relevance (practical utility and alignment), Clarity (comprehensibility and syntax), and Sufficiency (topical coverage).

4

Quantitative Scale: Use the numerical options from 1 to 4 available in the drop-down menus.

5

Qualitative Feedback: In 'Qualitative Comments and Suggestions', provide specific recommendations to refine wording, avoid leading bias, or adjust question tone.

ITEM RATING SCALE GUIDE (1 TO 4)

Score Meaning in Question Evaluation Validity Interpretation
1 Poor: The question is NOT relevant, NOT clear, or insufficient. Invalid Item (Requires elimination or major rewrite)
2 Fair: The question is barely relevant or needs major revisions. Invalid Item (Requires deep revision)
3 Good: The question is relevant and clear with minor adjustments. Valid Item (Approved with minor tweaks)
4 Excellent: The question is highly relevant, clear, and well-worded. Valid Item (Fully approved)
